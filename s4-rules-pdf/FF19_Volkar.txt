Special Setup
* Place the Track Action cards into the Action Tableau.
* Place the Attack Resolution card, the Damage tokens 	, and the Attack token 	next
to the play area.
* Shuffle the Movement cards and place the facedown deck next to the board. Then, deal out
one card facedown next to the deck, leaving room to deal out up to five additional cards.
* After revealing the Setup card, do not place the Killer meeple onto the board. Instead, place
the Killer meeple onto the back of the Finale card in the space indicated. Then, place an
Origin marker onto the space where the Killer would begin and place the second Origin
marker onto the back of the single facedown Movement card.
SPECIAL Rules
The Volk’ar has sophisticated cloaking technology and will spend much of the game hiding
in the shadows, hunting her prey. The Volk’ar does NOT have a Movement Value. Instead, her
movement will be directed by the path of Movement cards.
Definition of terms
* CONCEALED - The Volk’ar cannot be seen, although
she is ALWAYS somewhere on the board (indicated
by her Path - see below). When Concealed, her
current space is hidden and her Killer meeple is on
the Finale card. If the Volk'ar is Concealed at the
start of the Killer phase, resolve the upper Killer
Action on the Finale card.
* VISIBLE - The Volk’ar’s cloaking tech is disengaged
and she can be seen normally. Her Killer meeple will
be on the board when she is Visible. If the Volk'ar is
Visible at the start of the Killer phase, resolve the lower Killer Action on the Finale card.

Resolving the Volk'ar's path
The Volk’ar will resolve her Path and reveal herself when one of three things occurs:
* You resolve the Reveal icon 	on a card.
* You add a 6th Movement card to her Path. If this occurs while resolving the Killer Action
on the Finale card, finish resolving the Killer Action first before resolving her Path. If this
happens while resolving a Terror (or other) card, finish resolving the entire card first before
resolving her Path. This may result in there being more than 6 Movement cards to resolve.
* While Concealed, an attempted attack against her would do damage (see Attacking the
Volk'ar below).
When any of the above occurs and the Volk'ar is revealed, immediately do the following:
1. 	Place the Volk’ar’s Killer meeple onto the board in the space with the Origin marker,
even if that space is no longer accessible due to a location effect. Then, place both Origin
markers next to the Movement deck.
2. 	Starting with the Origin card, resolve each card in the Path one at a time, flipping the card
faceup if necessary. Follow the instructions on each Movement card to move the Volk’ar,
attacking in her current space if indicated. Any spaces with movement restrictions or
penalties for the Killer will apply to the Volk’ar and sometimes result in her not moving for
a particular Movement card.
3. 	If either you or Volk’ar take damage while resolving a Movement card and need to reveal
the Final Health token, wait until all the Movement cards have been resolved first.
4. 	After resolving all the cards in the Path, discard the Movement cards.
Tracking the Volk'ar
You can use the Track Action card in order to hone in on the exact space
where the Volk’ar is hiding. If all Movement cards in the Path are faceup,
then you will know EXACTLY which space the Volk'ar is in, but if even one
card is facedown, you will have to guess at her exact location.
Note! You are never allowed to look through the pile of discarded Movement cards.

ATTACKING THE VOLK’AR WHen she's CONCEALED
When Concealed, the Volk’ar is always at a specific space on the board, indicated by her Path.
Any space on the board within range can be targeted (following normal attack rules) AS IF the
Volk’ar was present there. Place the Attack token 	in the space being targeted and resolve the
attack. Track how much damage resulted from the attack by placing Damage tokens 	on the
Attack Resolution card. If the attack resulted in no damage, the Volk'ar remains Concealed and
her Path is not resolved. Otherwise, resolve the Volk'ar's Path as previously described.
If the Volk’ar’s final space is the same as the space with the 	token,
the attack's damage is resolved! Apply the damage on the Attack
Resolution card to the Volk’ar.
If the Volk’ar ended her movement in a different space, the attack had no
effect. Instead, the Volk’ar ambushes YOU (regardless of who the attack
came from). Resolve the Ambush Penalty on the bottom of the final
Movement card in her Path.
Finally, discard any 	tokens on the Attack Resolution card and
remove the 	token from the board.
When the VOLK’AR IS CONCEALED
If the Volk’ar is Concealed, the following exceptions apply:
* If an effect asks you to place something, or count spaces, in relation to the Killer, treat the
space with the Origin marker as the Volk’ar’s current space for the purposes of resolving
the effect.
* If an effect asks you to place the Killer, ignore the effect.
* If a card has a Killer Action line, treat it as a 	icon.
* Victims will not panic during the Panic phase.
Note! If you attacked your current space AND the Volk’ar ended her movement in that
space AND there was a Killer Attack line on the final Movement card in her Path, both your
attack and the Volk’ar’s are considered to have happened simultaneously.
