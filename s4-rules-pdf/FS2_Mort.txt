Special Setup
* Place the Basic Finale card on the Killer board. It will be used for the
Killer Action until the Finale is revealed.
* Add the Interrogate and Coerce Action cards to the Action Tableau.
* Place the 10 Suspect cards in a row above the board with their attributes side showing.
* Place the 10 Suspect tokens in the Suspect bag.
* Mort does not start on the board. Instead, he is wearing
one of 10 possible disguises. After placing the Victims
and Final Girl on the Location board, take the 10
Suspect miniatures and randomly place each Suspect
on an empty space (meaning there are no other
characters on the space) until there are either no empty spaces left or no Suspects left to
place. If there are no empty spaces and Suspects still need to be placed, place them in the
furthest spaces from you that contain Victims, but only 1 Suspect per space.
* Randomly choose a Clue card and place it next to the
board on whichever side you wish. This card determines
who Mort will be disguised as. Place the Clue Viewer and
the Reminder tokens, facedown, next to the Clue card.
* Place the Horror Track token on the
Killer board in its designated space.
Note! There is a special Finale Secret Envelope included with Mort.
DO NOT OPEN it until you are instructed to! Otherwise, it will spoil the surprise.

SPECIAL Rules
Suspects
You are attempting to figure out who Mort is actually masquerading as, but until you’ve eliminated
all Suspects but one, it could be anyone! There are 10 Suspects, each dressed up as a different
Final Girl villain. For gameplay purposes, Suspects are not considered the Killer, an Enemy, or a
Victim. They will never be targeted and cannot be damaged.
Suspects have 7 attributes, listed on their respective Suspect card. These attributes are what will
help you determine who Mort is actually disguised as.
Revealing Clues
There are two new Action cards: Interrogate and Coerce. These cards
can be used to reveal clues to identify which of the Suspects Mort is
NOT disguised as (see later in the rules for how these Action cards
are used and resolved). Whenever you successfully resolve either
Interrogate or Coerce and reveal a Clue, do the following:
1. Reveal one of the facedown Reminder tokens
(numbered 1-5).
2. Use the Clue Viewer to look at the
corresponding Clue on the Clue card. It will
indicate an attribute that Mort does NOT
possess.
3. Determine which Suspects cannot be Mort
by looking at their attributes and discarding
their Suspect cards from the lineup.
4. For each Suspect card discarded, remove
that Suspect miniature from the board
and replace it with an Informant meeple.
5. Keep the Reminder token faceup near
the Clue card so that you remember
which Clues you’ve already seen.
Revealing a Clue will ALWAYS eliminate at least
one Suspect, and may eliminate more than one.

Basic Finale card with the Suspect card. The Finale is now active. If the Dark Power hasn't been
revealed, reveal it immediately. Mort immediately receives the amount of health indicated on the
Suspect card (including a Final Health token).
Interrogate & Coerce
Interrogate can only be resolved while in a space with a Suspect. After successfully interrogating
a Suspect, they will panic.
Coerce can only be resolved while in a space with an Informant. After successfully coercing an
Informant, you will remove them from the board.
Important! When Mort's disguise has
been revealed, don't forget to always
remove any Informant meeples that
are still on the board!

* When you save an Informant, after receiving the reward from your Final Girl card, you may
then reduce Horror once ( 	).
* If an Informant is killed, after increasing Bloodlust, you must increase Horror once ( 	).
* When resolving 	or 	for the Active Killer (see below), follow normal rules but if there
are ties, prioritize Informants over normal Victims.
Suspects Temporarily Acting as Killers
Whenever you see this icon 	, draw a token out of the Suspect bag. The token will correspond
to one of the ten Suspects, who will then temporarily become the Active Killer. Mort is a master of
disguise and distraction and could be impersonating ANYONE, if only for a moment! Place the token
back into the bag after resolving the Killer Action(s). That Suspect could become the Active Killer
again on the same or future turn.
When drawing a token, if the indicated Suspect has already been proven to NOT be Mort (i.e.,
their Suspect card has been discarded because of a revealed Clue and is no longer in the lineup)
nothing happens and the Killer Action/effect is ignored. Instead, discard the token (do not place
it back in the bag).
For each Location card (e.g. Terror cards, Event cards, etc.) that requires you to resolve an action
or an effect for the Killer, draw a token from the bag as if there was a 	icon present on the card.

ALL Killer Actions/effects on that card.

Panic phase

RUNNING OUT OF TERROR CARDS BEFORE REVEALING ALL CLUES
If the Finale is revealed normally when the Terror deck is exhausted, flip the Clue card over to its
opposite side and reveal who Mort is disguised as. Replace that Suspect's miniature with Mort's
miniature and flip their Suspect card over, replacing the Basic Finale card with the Suspect card.
