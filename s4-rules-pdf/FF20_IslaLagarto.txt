Special Setup
* Place Electric Fence markers in the green sections
indicated on the Setup card. Do not place Electric Fence
markers in the red sections.
* Shuffle the 3 Lava cards and create a facedown deck.
Place the Lava tokens next to the deck.
* Place the T-Rex card near the play area and place the
T-Rex meeple on the start space.
SPECIAL rules
Electric Fences
The power to each electric fence fluctuates to keep the games exciting!
There are three Electric Fences (A, B, and C).
While an Electric Fence marker is present, movement on the path is blocked and the spaces on
either side of the fence are NOT considered adjacent.
* Panic - if anyone panics in a direction blocked by a fence, they simply stay in their current
space.
* Farthest/Closest – when determining distance to a space or target, only use paths that are
not blocked by Electric Fence markers. If all markers are on the board and the only space
or target is on the other side of the fences, determine distance as if there were no Electric
Fence markers.
* Enemy Targeting – if there is no unblocked path between an Enemy and its target, then
the Enemy will not move as part of that action. Attack icons will still be resolved in the
Enemy’s space.
* Placing someone – effects that place someone are not affected by fences.
During each Upkeep phase, the fence power fluctuates. Roll the Electric Fence die to determine
which fence is affected. If there’s an Electric Fence marker already there, remove it from the
board. If there isn’t an Electric Fence marker there, place one.

T-Rex
The T-Rex begins the game secured in the Dino Pen but it may eventually break free!
When you see the 	icon, move the T-Rex meeple one space to the right on the T-Rex card.
When the T-Rex reaches the “Break out!” space, place the T-Rex meeple on the board in the Dino
Pen, and place the T-Rex Action token below the Killer Action on the Finale card. Ignore any
subsequent 	icons as the T-Rex is now in play.
The T-Rex is NOT considered a Killer or a Minion and cannot be attacked, damaged, or killed. After
resolving the Minion and Killer Actions on the Finale card, resolve the T-Rex Action.
Victims will not follow you into the T-Rex’s space (unless there is an effect that allows them to
follow you into the Killer’s space). During the Panic phase, everyone in the T-Rex’s space panics,
regardless of whether or not a Victim was killed that turn.
Moving
The T-Rex's Movement Value is on the T-Rex card (it's a 2). While moving, the 	icon means
the T-Rex will stop moving as soon as it reaches a space with ANYONE (e.g. you, Victims,
Enemies, etc.). It will also NOT move if anyone is in its starting space.
Killing
The T-Rex doesn't attack like a normal Enemy. Instead of doing
damage, the T-Rex instantly KILLS one target in its space! If there
are multiple targets, it will kill ONE of them using the following
priority: Victim; Minion; You; Killer. If you
or the Killer were attacked by the T-Rex,
you (or they) are instantly killed; do not
reveal any Final Health
tokens.

Volcano
The first time you resolve a 	icon, the volcano erupts! Follow these steps:
1. 	Everyone at the Volcano takes 	.
2. 	Anyone left in the space panics.
3. 	Place the Volcano Lava token in that space on the board. It is no longer accessible to
anyone!
When you resolve subsequent 	icons, the lava from the volcano will spread to an adjacent
space. Draw a Lava card to determine which space and follow the instructions on the card.
After all 4 Lava tokens are on the board, ignore any subsequent 	icons. Spaces with Lava
tokens are no longer accessible to anyone.
