billy's buddies
Buddies are Minions and follow normal Minion rules (familiarize
yourself with the Minion rules on pg. 32 of the Core Rulebook).
Each Buddy card represents an animatronic with their own
health ( 	), Attack Value ( 	), and Movement Value ( 	).
spawning a buddy
When an effect says to “Spawn a Buddy”, reveal the top card of
the Buddy deck. Place the card in the play area and place the
indicated number of Health markers on top of the Buddy card.
Place the Buddy’s token in any space adjacent to Billy, choosing
a space that does not already have a Buddy if possible.
Note! If Billy is no longer in play the effect is ignored (no Buddy spawns).
Activating Buddies
Each Buddy card has an Initiative number found in the circle in the top right corner of the card.
When resolving Minion Actions and/or Terror card effects, resolve them in the order of the
Initiative number from lowest to highest.
Note! If a Buddy spawns during the Killer phase, it will not activate until the NEXT Killer phase.
killing Buddies
When a Buddy is killed, remove the Buddy’s token from the board. Take the Kill Reward indicated
on the Buddy’s card and then remove the card from the game. If a Buddy is killed in a phase other
than the Action phase, take the reward at the beginning of the next Action phase.
attacking billy
IMPORTANT! Buddies will protect Billy at all costs. This means that Billy cannot be attacked
while there is a Buddy in his space.
