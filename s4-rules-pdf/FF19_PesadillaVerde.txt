SPECIAL rules
THE SOLDIERS
Every normal Victim is a Soldier, deployed to the conflict zone of Pesadilla Verde and eager to
fight! They act like normal Victims for almost all game effects. Unlike normal Victims, Soldiers
WILL follow you into the Killer's space and they can be given orders (see Ordering Soldiers).
upgrades
There are two types of Upgrades: Action Upgrades (indicated by tokens with blank squares)
and Ability Upgrades (those without squares). At the beginning of the game, Soldiers only have
access to two Action Upgrades: moving and attacking. However, the Train Action card can be
used to Unlock additional Upgrades.
When you Unlock an Upgrade, choose an Upgrade token and flip it from its Locked side 	to
its Unlocked side. If a game effect tells you to Lock an Upgrade, flip it from its Unlocked side to
its Locked side.
There are 4 Action Upgrades, indicated by the tokens with
blank squares:
* Attack: You begin the game with one of these Unlocked
and one Locked. Using this Action will allow a Soldier or group of Soldiers to attack
Enemies in a space up to their range.
* Move: You begin the game with one of these Unlocked and one Locked. Using this Action
will allow a group of Soldiers to move 1 space.

There are 4 Ability Upgrades, indicated by the tokens
WITHOUT any squares:
* Saving Soldiers: Soldiers are very stubborn and do
not want to be “rescued.” They want to fight! At the start
of the game it costs 2 	to save a Soldier. If Unlocked, you can save Soldiers for no time
loss.
* Friendly Fire: At the start of the game, your Soldiers are not very skilled. Whenever you use
an Attack action, they will potentially shoot one another. Roll a die for each Soldier involved
in the attack. On a roll of 1-2, you must kill one of the Soldiers involved in the attack. If
Unlocked, there will no longer be any friendly fire when they attack.
* Attack Range: At the start of the game, Soldiers can only attack their current space. If
Unlocked, they can attack either their current space or an adjacent space.
* Damage: At the start of the game, when Soldiers attack they do 	equal to the number
of Soldiers divided by 2, rounded DOWN. This means that a single Soldier by itself cannot
do any damage, and three Soldiers together can only do 1 	. If Unlocked, when
Soldiers attack they will do 	equal to the number of Soldiers divided by 2, rounded UP.
Now a single Soldier will do 1 	and three Soldiers together will do 2 	.
Ordering Soldiers
Soldiers can be ordered to take Actions during the Action phase as follows:
* Select the space with the Soldier or Soldiers to be ordered.
* Choose an Action Upgrade token (the tokens with blank squares) and place an Action
cube on the leftmost space. An Action is not available if there’s already a cube in the row
for that Action.
* Resolve the Action for all Soldiers in that space.
During the Upkeep phase, move all Action cubes one space to the right, removing them from
the Action Upgrade token when they leave the last space. Once a cube has left the token it is
available again to use.
Note! The Archeologist and the Rebel Leader Special Victims are NOT considered
Soldiers and cannot be ordered to take Actions!

the huey
The space with the Huey token is considered a 	space. When you are in the same space as the Huey,
you can save any number of Victims there. Then, remove the Huey token from the board, revealing the
Clearing, which is not a 	space. Unless a game effect returns the Huey token to the board, you will
not be able to use it to save Victims again.
the River
There are four River spaces on the board: the Waterfall, the Upper
River, the Central River, and the Lower River. When moving
downriver (following the arrows), you can move to any space on
the River with a movement of 1 space . For example, if you’re at the
Waterfall and resolve a Walk Action card with a single success,
you could move all the way to the Lower River if you wished. When
moving from one River space to a non-adjacent River space, you are
considered to be passing through all spaces in between and must
resolve any effects on those spaces.
River movement also applies to Soldiers but not to Enemies. In other words, when ordering Soldiers
to move on the river, they may move across multiple River spaces for 1 space of movement. Enemies,
however, can only move downriver to an adjacent River space.
No one (you, Victims, or Enemies) can move upriver against the direction of the arrows. However, the
space upriver is still considered adjacent.
