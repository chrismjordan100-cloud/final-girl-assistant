* Place a Malice token in the Killer’s starting space and in 3 adjacent spaces
to the Killer. If there are only 2 adjacent spaces, then place one in the next
closest space of your choice. There will be 4 Malice tokens on the board.
* Place the Donkey token in the Cloister space.
* Place the Benediction Action cards in the Action Tableau.
