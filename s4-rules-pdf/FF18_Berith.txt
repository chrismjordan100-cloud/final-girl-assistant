SPECIAL Setup
* Place the Divine Blessings sheet next to the play area. Choose which side of the sheet you
want to use: Heavenly (normal) or Hellish (hard).
* Place the Expel Demon Action cards in the Action Tableau.
SPECIAL Rules
Berith’s Possession of Ursula
The demon Berith has taken corporeal form by possessing Ursula, a nun. He is controlling her
actions and shedding innocent blood through her. The Killer meeple as well as the health, Killer
Attack and Movement Values all represent Ursula’s physical body possessed by Berith. Once
possessed Ursula is "killed," Berith will leave her body.
DIVINE Blessings
When you see the 	icon, place a black Final Health token face down on the topmost empty
space on the Divine Blessings sheet and resolve the corresponding effect. This will be most
commonly done by resolving the Expel Demon Action card. Divine blessings could help restore
Ursula’s life after Berith has been expelled from her body.
Final Health tokens on the Divine Blessings sheet cannot be removed or revealed unless
specifically stated otherwise. The number of Final Health tokens is limited so there will be fewer
to put on the Divine Blessings sheet if any are removed from the game by other effects, assigned
to the Killer, or assigned to the Final Girl.
Winning the Game
You must first kill possessed Ursula following the normal rules, including revealing the black
Final Health token and restoring her health if necessary. Once possessed Ursula has been
defeated, Berith leaves her lifeless body. To win the game, Ursula must come back from the dead.
Reveal each Final Health token on the Divine Blessings sheet. If any show health, Ursula’s life
is restored and you win! If all of them are blank, Ursula's soul is condemned to hell and you lose.
Note! You don't need to fill the Divine Blessing sheet with all 7 black Final
Health tokens before defeating possessed Ursula. However, the more
blessings you've bestowed, the more likely she is to come back to life.
