Special Setup
* Shuffle the Spawn cards and form a face down deck. Place it near the play area along with
the Raptors card and place the Raptor meeples on the Raptors card.
* Place the Pack meeple on the Killer starting space 	indicated on the Setup card. It is
used instead of the Killer meeple from the Core box.
SPECIAL Rules
The Pack
The Pack meeple represents a large and coordinated group of Raptors. The Pack is considered a
Killer for all purposes.
The health of the Pack represents the number of Raptors in the Pack. Health starts the game at
5 (one Final Health token and 4 Health markers), but there is no upper limit. The Attack Value on
the Killer board represents how much damage the Pack does in a single attack, regardless of the
size of the pack.
When the Pack is eliminated, you win, even if there are still individual Raptors on the board.
Individual Raptors
Individual Raptors are represented by the Raptor meeples. When a card refers to ‘Raptor(s)’ for
an effect, that effect does NOT include the Pack, only the individual Raptors. Similarly, when a
card refers to ‘Pack’ for an effect, it does not include individual Raptors.
Raptors are considered Minions for all purposes (familiarize yourself with the Minion rules on
pg. 32 of the Core Rulebook).
* Individual Raptors have 1 health.
* They use the Movement Value on the Killer board.
* Individual Raptors don’t attack very often, but when they do, they EACH attack individually
for 1 	.
JOINING THE PACK
Raptors are generally trying to move to, and join, the Pack. When you see the 	icon, the
Raptors are targeting the Pack for movement.

Raptors know they are at risk when not with the Pack, so when moving they will
stop any time they enter (or begin movement in) a space with you or a Victim. This is
reflected by the 	icon as a reminder.
When targeting the Pack, the Raptors will take the shortest path to reach it. If there are multiple
equidistant paths, the Raptors will choose the path that gets them as close to the Pack as possi-
ble before having to stop their movement.
When individual Raptors are in the same space as the Pack, they automatically and immediately
join it. Simply remove the individual Raptors and add one Health marker to the Pack’s health for
each one that joins.
When Raptor meeples are removed from the board, place them on the Raptors card to be used
again for future spawns.
Spawning Raptors
When you resolve an 	icon, you must draw a Spawn card and spawn the indicated number of
individual Raptors. The space they spawn in is determined by rolling a die and referring to the
spawn table on the Raptors card. If there are multiple legal spawn spaces, you may choose where
to spawn the Raptors. Then, discard the Spawn card.
* Spawns cannot occur in spaces that are no longer accessible.
* If there are not enough Raptor meeples to resolve the spawn, the Raptor closest to the
Pack immediately joins it, and then use that Raptor meeple for the spawn. Repeat this until
the Spawn card is fully resolved.
* If you ever need to draw a Spawn card and none remain, shuffle the discard pile to create a
new deck and then draw.
* The final effect on the Bloodlust track will also spawn a Raptor. Resolve this as if you had
drawn a Spawn card with a value of 1.
Note! Location Terror cards will not have this icon, but it still applies
when individual Raptors resolve the 	icon!
Note! If a Raptor spawns during the Killer phase, it will not activate
until the NEXT Killer phase.
