Special Setup
* Shuffle the 12 Booth cards and make a facedown Booth deck.
* Shuffle the 10 Booth Event cards and make a facedown Booth Event deck.
* Add the 2 Create a Panic Action cards to the Action Tableau.
* Place the Finale Reminder card underneath the Finale card
(Finale Reminder side facing up). It will only be used after the
Finale is revealed.
SPECIAL Rules
DEFINITIONS
* EXHIBIT AREAS: The yellow spaces marked with this icon 	are considered Exhibit
Area spaces. Thematically this is where the vendor booths are and where everyone wants
to be!
* FOOD COURT: The Food Court is located in the bottom left-hand corner of the board.
* GENERAL AREAS: All remaining spaces not captured in the 2 areas above are considered
the General Areas.
NOTE! See the back of this rulebook for a visual key to each of these areas on the board.
Restricted Victim following
The Victims are having such a great time at the convention that they will not willingly follow you
OUT of Exhibit Areas and into General Areas! You may not have ANY Victims follow you when
moving from a 	space to a General Area space. Instead, you will need to use the Create a
Panic Action card to attempt to scare them from a 	space to a General Area space. Victims
could also panic and move from a 	space to a General Area space per the normal rules (i.e. if
panicking during the Panic phase or panicking from other effects).
Main hallway
When panicking someone from the Main Hallway, roll 2 dice instead of 1 to determine the
direction they panic.

Searching
At Mega Boardgame Con you can search in ANY space (by resolving a Search Action card). The
Item deck you draw from depends on the kind of space you are searching in: an Exhibit Area
space 	; the Food Court space; or a General Area space.
MOVING THROUGH Hallways and docks
Hallways and docks are sparsely populated and you can move to other connected hallways and
docks quickly! If you intend to move to a space connected by this icon 	, you get +1
to resolve the Action card that grants the movement, but the first space you move to MUST be an
adjacent space connected with a 	icon.
booth cards and booth events
During each Upkeep phase you will draw a Booth card. If you need to draw a Booth card but the
deck is empty, shuffle the discard pile to create a new deck.
When resolving a Booth card, roll a die and do one of the following:
* 1-3: Spawn a normal Victim in the space with the Booth. This represents a new attendee
showing up to the convention!
* 4-6: Draw a Booth Event card and apply its effects in the space with the Booth.
Discard the Booth card after resolving it. Booth Event cards will instruct you as to whether or not
the card should be discarded.
NOTE! Some card effects will instruct you to draw a Booth card for determining a Booth location.
In these instances, do not resolve the Booth card by rolling a die.
IMPORTANT! Because all spaces can be searched, there are no 	icons on the spaces.
However, each space is considered a 	space for the purpose of effects that reference
them. If unsure what to do in certain instances, apply the Rules on Game Ambiguity.
IMPORTANT! You will no longer draw a Booth card after the
Finale is revealed. Take the Finale Reminder card, flip it over, and
place it on top of the Booth deck. 	Do not draw Booth cards during
	the Upkeep phase any longer.
