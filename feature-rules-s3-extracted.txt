special rules
the savior
The Savior is a young teenager upon whom the fate of the human race depends. It is your job to protect him,
because if he dies then you immediately lose the game. However, the Savior is reckless and mistrusting and
refuses to believe you until you’ve built enough trust with him. The Savior is considered an Ally (see Ally rules
at the end).
TARGETING THE SAVIOR and losing the game
The Savior is the Killer’s primary target. When an Enemy would target you 	, it instead targets the Savior.
These targeting rules are in addition to those mentioned in the Ally rules at the end. If the Savior dies you
immediately lose the game.
Building trust with the savior
There is a new Action card called Build Trust which can only be used when
you are in the same space as the Savior. This will allow you to increase his
Trust level on the Trust card. Increasing Trust moves the marker up while
decreasing Trust moves it down. The Trust level will affect how much control
you have over the Savior and how he will act of his own accord.

ACtive Killer
The Active Killer at the start of the game is the Mark V model. Near the end of the game, the Mark X model will
appear and become the Active Killer. If the rules or an effect refer to the Hunter, it applies to whichever model
is the Active Killer.
BLOODLUST
Since the Hunter is focused on its primary target, it derives no satisfaction from the deaths of bystanders. So,
unlike other Killers, Bloodlust does not increase when a Victim is killed. Instead, Bloodlust increases when the
Hunter either deals damage to you or an Ally or is damaged itself. This represents the Hunter’s cybernetic brain
learning from these attacks and becoming smarter after each clash.
Bloodlust can only be increased this way once per phase. For example, if you were to play a Retaliate card and
both you AND the Hunter damaged each other, Bloodlust would still only go up once that phase.
PANIC PHASE
During the Panic phase, in addition to normal panicking rules, Victims in the Hunter’s space also panic if Bloodlust
increased that turn.
defeating the mark v
The Final Health token and Health markers on the Killer board are for the Mark X and do NOT apply to the Mark
V. Instead, in order to defeat the Mark V you must destroy each of its Cybernetic Parts. It takes 2 	to remove
a single part. Damage can accumulate with multiple attacks but does not carry over to subsequent phases. For
example, you can perform a Weak Attack to deal 	and then a Furious Strike to deal another 	, destroying
a Cybernetic Part. If you had instead ended the Action phase without performing the Furious Strike, then the
single 	from the Weak Attack would have been wasted.
special rules
Multiple Cybernetic Parts can be removed with one or multiple attacks in the same
phase. For example, if your combined attacks in a single phase did 5 	, then 2
Cybernetic Parts are destroyed.
When a Cybernetic Part is destroyed, remove the top Cybernetic Part token from
the back of the Finale card. Then, roll a die and place the Cybernetic Part token
in or adjacent to the Mark V’s space according to the number rolled and the Panic
numbers on the Mark V’s space.
The Mark V is defeated and shuts down once all of the Cybernetic Part tokens have
been removed from the Finale card. Leave the Mark V’s meeple where it was on the
Location board. Then, take the Mark V’s Dark Power card, revealing it if it’s still face
down, and slide it under the Ally card so that the special ability is showing at the
bottom. If the Mark V was defeated during the Action phase, you may continue your
turn. Otherwise, the phase immediately ends. For all game effects, the Mark V is no
longer considered a Killer or Enemy.
countdown tokens
When all the Cybernetic Part tokens have been removed and only Countdown tokens remain on top of the
Finale card, ignore the Killer action on the back of the Finale card and do not draw a Terror card. Instead,
remove a Countdown token at the beginning of the Killer phase and increase Bloodlust by 1. When the second
Countdown token is removed (as well as when resolving the Hunter Mark X Terror card), draw a new Setup
card and place the silver Killer meeple for the Mark X in the Killer starting space indicated on that card. The
Mark X is now the Active Killer. On the following turn, resolve the Killer phase as normal.
RETRIEVING CYBERNETIC PARTS

while in the token’s space (you cannot do this while moving through the space). If there are multiple tokens on the
space, each token requires you to spend 	. Keep the token facedown when taking it and place it in your Backpack.
Cybernetic Parts may be picked up even before the Mark V has been defeated.
REPAIRING THE MARK V
Once you have one or more Cybernetic Parts in your Backpack and the Mark V has been defeated, you may
begin re-installing them on the Mark V to repair it. While in a space with the Mark V, you may spend 	to flip 1

hellscape tiles
The Hellscape is ever-changing. There are 4 double-sided tiles, each with one of the following symbols: 	,
, 	, and 	. The position of the tiles will change throughout the game. Sometimes this will be done at your
whim using the Hellshift Action card or an Item, and sometimes it will be done outside of your control due to a
game effect. When a tile is moved, all pieces (Final Girl, Enemies, Victims, Tokens, etc.) remain on the tile, so you
will have to be very careful when moving the tiles around!
* Shifting Tiles - if an effect says to Shift, you will move each tile in the direction indicated (clockwise or
counter-clockwise).
* Swapping Tiles - if an effect says to Swap, the chosen or indicated tiles will trade places.
* Flipping Tiles - if an effect says to Flip, take the tile and remove all pieces from the tile, noting
which colored space they were in. Then flip the tile and return the pieces to it, placing them in their
corresponding colored spaces.
* Rearranging Tiles - if an effect says to Rearrange, you may arrange ALL the tiles on the board to
whatever configuration you choose. Tiles cannot be Flipped when Rearranging.
hellscape dice
Once during each Upkeep phase, you must roll the Hellscape dice and do the following:
* If the symbols match, Flip the indicated tile.
* If the symbols are different, Swap the indicated tiles.
WALLS AND ADJACENCY
Each Hellscape tile has one or two sides which are a wall that cannot be moved through. Depending on the
current layout of the tiles, there will often be times where a tile or section of tiles will be completely blocked off
from you and/or any Enemies. You’ll need to manipulate the tiles or wait for them to change if you need to get to
one of those areas.
* Panic - if a Victim (or anyone) panics into a wall/unconnected direction, they simply stay in their current
space.

* Killer Targeting and Range – range and targeting are always done by calculating the number of spaces
required to move from the source to the target. If there is no valid path to the target of a Killer Action,
then the Killer will not move as part of that action—attack icons will still be resolved in the Killer’s space.
* Farthest/Closest – some effects will have you determine the farthest or closest Victim to you, or to the
Killer. When making this determination, only consider Victims that are reachable through paths not
blocked by walls. If no Victims can be reached through unblocked paths, then ignore walls on Hellscape
tiles that are blocking paths and use your best judgment to determine the specified Victim. This also
applies if you’re determining specific spaces or objects (e.g. nearest Exit space).
* Placing someone – effects that place the Killer, Final Girl, or Victims are not affected by walls. The term
“place” is different than “move”—"place” ignores walls, while “move” does not.

Afflictions
Hellish Afflictions can be physical, mental, spiritual, or otherwise. To be unbound from the influence and power
of Razorface, you must Purge all of your Afflictions (usually 3, but you may gain more during the game) so that
you can kill Razorface and escape with your life and soul intact.
* Purging an Affliction – each Affliction card has a Purge Requirement that you must achieve to
Purge that Affliction. During the Action phase, if the Purge Requirement has been met, you may
choose to Purge the Affliction by resolving the Purge Effect on the bottom half of the card. If you
choose not to Purge the Affliction when the requirement is met, then the Purge Requirement needs
to be achieved again later to Purge that Affliction. Once an Affliction has been Purged, remove the
Affliction from the game.
* Impossible Afflictions – it could happen that, during
the course of play, a particular Affliction becomes
impossible to Purge (due to there being no Victims on
the board, for example). When this occurs, remove the
Affliction from the game and draw a new one to replace
it. You immediately lose the game if you need to draw an
Affliction and none remain.
Final Health Token
Razorface’s Final Health token may only be revealed if there are NO Afflictions in play. As such, when Razorface
is down to its Final Health token and one or more Afflictions are still in play, damage to Razorface that would
normally reveal the Final Health token is instead ignored.

definitions
* BRIDGE: Refers to the paths with roads that cross the river and the Bridge token (if it has been placed
on the board).
* RIVER CROSSING: Refers to the paths with blue lines that cross the river.
river crossings
You will normally not be able to use River Crossings, but some effects will allow you to use them. Victims may
follow you as normal. Enemies cannot use River Crossings. The spaces connected by a River Crossing are not
considered adjacent nor connected for the purpose of determining range (or when resolving effects that refer
to adjacent spaces).
missions
Each Mission sheet will have specific instructions on how to setup and complete the Mission. Completing the
Mission is mandatory to win the game. The Killer can only lose their final health and reveal their Final Health
token after the Mission is completed. Any damage that would reveal the Killer’s Final Health token before the
Mission is completed is ignored. If there is more than one Killer, this only applies to the last remaining Killer.
Some cards will instruct you to Gain Progress or Lose Progress on the Mission. Refer to the Mission sheet for
an explanation on how to resolve this and place a Tracking marker on the marked space as a reminder of the
progress effect.
friends
When you complete a Mission, a Friend will join you to help you defeat the Killer. Friends are considered Special
Victims but have three important differences:
* Friends will follow you into the Killer’s space.
* Other Victims in the same space as a Friend will be attacked before Friends are attacked.
* Friends will not panic if they are in your space.
