## Quickest way to do it
- **Total photos:** about 49 card sides (19 backs + 30 × 2), but you don't photograph cards one by one. Lay out 6–8 cards face up on a dark surface, take one photo straight down, flip them all in place and take a second. That's roughly 12–14 photos for the lot.
- Send them to Claude and say which box(es) they're from. Claude will cut out, straighten and name each card and save it into the project.

## Sources checked
- Fan wiki (finalgirl.fandom.com): all 42 Final Girl pages and all 306 uploaded images.
- Van Ryder Games shop: every Final Girl product's image gallery.
- Feature Film rulebooks (Series 1–3) and the Series 4 rules sheets: these show no Final Girl cards.
- Kickstarter campaign pages: only small composite images, not usable.
- BoardGameGeek: blocked by its bot check, so not searched. Its user photo galleries are worth a manual look if you want to avoid some photos.
