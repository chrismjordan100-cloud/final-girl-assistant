# Season 4 — what to do when the boxes arrive

Plain-English checklist for you. No code, no jargon. Your job is mostly to
**take photos** and **hand them to Kiro**; Kiro does the building.

Most of Season 4 is already in the app (the killers, locations, final girls,
setup steps, play rules and rule-symbol icons are done). What's left all needs
things that are physically in the boxes — the actual cards, the artwork, and a
couple of sheets Kiro hasn't seen yet.

---

## The short version

For each of the six boxes, you'll:

1. Lay the cards and boards out.
2. Photograph them (clear, well-lit, straight-on).
3. Give the photos to Kiro and say what they are.

That's basically it. The rest is Kiro's job.

The six boxes:

- **Bad Times at Buddyland** — Billy the Bear / Buddyland
- **A Rotten Harvest** — Grimlash / Shady Acres
- **A Demon in the Shadows** — Berith / L'Armes Abbey
- **The Green Terror** — The Volk'ar / Pesadilla Verde
- **The Raptor Games** — The Raptors / Isla Lagarto
- **Shriek (Special Feature)** — Mort / Mega Board Game Con

Plus two older ones if you have them: **North Pole Nightmare** (Krampus /
Santa's Village) and the **Terror From Beyond** vignette.

---

## Before you start — a few photo tips

These make a big difference to how much Kiro can read without guessing:

- **Good light, no shadow, phone straight above the card** (not at an angle).
- **One thing in focus per photo.** A whole deck fanned out is fine for an
  overview, but also take close-ups of any card with small text.
- **Fill the frame.** Get close enough that the rules text is sharp.
- **Name the shot when you send it** — e.g. "Buddyland item cards" or
  "Grimlash killer board front". Kiro can't always tell which box a loose card
  came from.
- If a card's text is blurry, **retake it** rather than hoping — Kiro will flag
  anything it can't read rather than invent it, so a clear photo saves a round trip.

## Step 1 — The card reference (the big one)

This builds the searchable list of every card. Do it **one box at a time** so
things don't get mixed up.

For each box, spread out and photograph each of these piles (keep them separate):

- **Terror cards** — keep the **Killer's** Terror cards and the **Location's**
  Terror cards in two separate piles, and tell Kiro which is which.
- **Setup cards**
- **Item cards** — if a card shows which search space or item deck it belongs to,
  try to keep those grouped so that's visible.
- **Event cards**
- **The killer's special cards:** Dark Power, Epic Dark Power, Finale, Epic Finale.
- **Any killer-specific extras**, e.g. Buddy cards (Billy), Harvest Madness cards
  (Grimlash), Raptor Spawn cards (Raptors), Volk'ar Movement cards, Mort's
  Clue/Suspect cards, etc.

Then send the photos to Kiro: *"Here are the [box name] cards — build the Series 4
card reference for this film."* Kiro will read each card (name, flavour text, full
effect, stats like range/damage, and any rules notes) and flag anything it can't
read clearly.

---

## Step 2 — The artwork and board images

These are the pictures the app shows on the Codex and "This Game" screens. For
each killer and location, photograph:

- **The key art** (the big character/location picture).
- **The flavour-text panel** (the little story blurb on the sheet).
- **Each page of the rules sheet** (front and back, in order).
- **The small round emblem / icon** for the killer and the location (the little
  badge symbol).

For each final girl:

- **The front and back** of her card.

For each official pairing, if there's a **scenario title graphic** (the big film
title image), photograph that too.

Send them to Kiro: *"Here's the art and board images for [box name]."* Kiro files
them so they appear automatically.

---

## Step 3 — The achievements

Each box has an **achievements card or sheet** (the list of "Defeat X here",
"win without losing health", that sort of thing). Kiro hasn't seen these — they
aren't in the rulebooks.

Photograph the achievement list for each killer and location, and send them:
*"Here are the achievements for [box name]."* Kiro adds them as the end-of-game
tick boxes.

---

## Step 4 — The two boxes Kiro is still missing rules for

If you have them:

- **North Pole Nightmare** (Krampus / Santa's Village) — Kiro has the cards but
  not the rules. Photograph the **killer rules sheet** and the **location rules
  sheet** (all pages) and send them: *"Here are the North Pole Nightmare rules."*
- **Terror From Beyond** (the Series 4 vignette — the plant-pod one) — Kiro only
  has a placeholder for it. Photograph its **rules sheet** and send: *"Here's the
  Terror From Beyond vignette — add it properly."*

---

## Step 5 — Let Kiro check it, then it goes live

Once Kiro has built everything from your photos:

- Open the app and have a look (the **Asset Admin** screen shows a tick list —
  green ticks mean an image is in, red crosses mean one's still missing, so it's
  an easy way to spot gaps).
- Tell Kiro to **publish it** — it pushes the update and your phone picks it up a
  minute or two later (close and reopen the app shortcut to force it).
