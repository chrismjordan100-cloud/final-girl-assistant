# Final Girl – Add-on Box Rules (for the Rules Reference)

Prepared 5 Nov 2026 for the Final Girl Rule Assistant. This covers the rules
that come from add-on boxes rather than the Core Box or Feature Films.

**Confidence key** (store this on each topic as `confidence`):
- **official** – from Van Ryder Games' own rulebook PDF.
- **wiki** – from the Final Girl fan wiki (finalgirl.fandom.com).
- **fan-source** – from a fan rules summary (Redesign Insert). Treat as provisional.
- **unknown** – the card or rule exists but its text isn't published anywhere I
  could find. Chris will photograph the cards later. Do NOT invent text for these.

## 1. Box of Props (Series 2 Kickstarter, later retail) — official rulebook

Source: Box of Props Rulebook PDF, linked from vanrydergames.com/pages/final-girl-resources.
Every module is optional and works with any Killer/Location.

### 1.1 Upgrade components (no rules change)
- Horror Track miniature replaces the red Killer meeple on the Horror Track.
- Deluxe Time marker replaces the Time marker.
- Deluxe Bloodlust marker replaces the round Bloodlust marker.
- 12 Deluxe Final Health tokens replace the cardboard Final Health tokens.

### 1.2 The Desperation Die (official)
- Black die: 5 faces show a success, 1 face shows a skull.
- **Once per game**, you may swap ONE of your red dice for the Desperation Die
  for a single Horror Roll. Roll it with any other red dice your Horror Level gives you.
- Success face: add 1 success to the roll's total.
- Skull face: **you immediately lose the game.**
- Meant only for truly desperate moments (e.g. the last hit you need).

### 1.3 Book of Desperate Deaths (official, flavour only)
- If the Desperation Die kills you, look up the Action card you were trying to
  resolve in the booklet. It gives a paragraph describing how you died.
- No rules effect. Fits the app's existing death-narration feature.

### 1.4 Signature Action cards (official setup rule)
- **Setup:** before the game, choose (or randomly pick) **ONE** Signature Action
  card. Put it in the Action card Tableau as an extra card you can buy.
- It works like any other Action card: it cycles back into the Tableau after use,
  and can be discarded for Time or to convert, and so on.
- Van Ryder recommends using one every game once you're comfortable with the
  standard Action cards (Series 3 Kickstarter update #6).
- The Box of Props contains **10** Signature Action cards.

Card list. Only Dodge's text is confirmed by the official rulebook; the rest are fan-source:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 1.5 Ultimate Dice (official)
Six custom dice, plus an Ultimate Dice card showing which dice you can take.

**Earning a die**
- When you save enough Victims to unlock your Final Girl's Ultimate Ability, you
  may INSTEAD take an Ultimate Die. Move your saved Victims onto the Ultimate Dice card.
- 0–3 Victims on the card: not yet eligible. Keep saving until you have at least 4.
  The rulebook says this currently only matters for Julia.
- 4 Victims: take ONE Tier 1 die.
- 5 Victims: take ONE Tier 1 or Tier 2 die.
- 6 Victims: take ONE die from ANY Tier.
- You may delay taking a die to reach a higher Tier. But once Victims are committed
  to the Ultimate Dice card, you do **not** get your normal Ultimate Ability.
- Once you take a die, it's yours for the rest of the game.

**Using a die**
- When resolving an eligible Action card, roll your Ultimate Die **in addition to**
  the red dice from your Horror Level, and add its effect.
- You normally only get the bonus if the Action card's result already produces an
  effect of that type. Example: a failed Weak Attack does no damage, so the Damage Die adds nothing.
- Use judgement for Signature Actions, location-specific Actions and future cards.

| Tier | Die | Used with | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Health | Short Rest, Long Rest | +1 Health per heart rolled (the card must grant at least 1 Health) |
| 1 | Search | Search | Draw 1 extra Item card per card icon, giving more choice; you still only take the normal number |
| 2 | Time | Focus, Distraction | Gain Time per Time icon, even if the card result loses Time (they offset) |
| 2 | Movement | Walk, Sprint | +1 space per movement icon (the card must grant at least 1 space) |
| 3 | Horror | Focus, Distraction, Furious Strike, Critical Blow | Extra Horror reduction per icon (the card must reduce Horror by at least 1) |
| 3 | Damage | Weak Attack, Furious Strike, Retaliate, Critical Blow | +damage per icon (the card must deal at least 1 damage) |

**Optional variants (official)**
- **Penultimate Dice:** start with one Ultimate Die of your choice (easier game).
- **Upgrade Dice:** whenever you save a Victim after choosing your die, you may swap it for another type.
- **Ultimate Desperation:** remove your Ultimate Die from the game to use the Desperation Die a second time.

### 1.6 Promo Final Girls in the Box of Props
4 Final Girl cards: Layla Khaira (5 Health), Agnes Crane (4 Health), Julia Paris (6 Health),
Constance Moreau (5 Health). Julia and Constance are guest characters from VRG's *The Dark Quarter*.
- **Layla** (official): her Ultimate uses the Signature Action cards. Fan-source detail:
  she takes an out-of-play Signature Action card into her hand.
- **Agnes** (official): her Ultimate uses the Ultimate Dice. Fan-source detail: she
  gains 2 Ultimate Dice of her choice without the Victim-based unlock.
- **Julia** (fan-source): may kill a Victim in her space to choose one of: recover all
  Health; damage an Enemy and recover Health; or move 2 spaces ignoring movement
  penalties. This raises the Killer's Bloodlust. She is the one Final Girl who can
  sit at 0–3 Victims for Ultimate Dice purposes (official).
- **Constance** (fan-source): may use any weapon in her hand at range 0–2.

Unlockable envelope items (wiki): Layla's Sickle, Agnes' Magnum, Julia's Ancient
Dagger, Constance's Voodoo Doll. The Guest Stars box contains these as item cards.

## 2. Guest Stars Box (Series 3) — wiki contents; most card text unknown

Contents (wiki and retailer listings):
- **Carolyn's Revenge** character pack and **Grandma's Grudge** character pack (see 2.1, 2.2).
- Final Girl cards: Agnes, Layla (reprints from Box of Props), Carolyn, Grandma,
  Jamie (promo), Paula (Holiday version).
- Item cards: Agnes' Magnum, Constance's Voodoo Doll, Gabrielle's Crystal Ball of Blood,
  Grandma's Handbag, Jamie's Hockey Stick, Julia's Ancient Dagger, Layla's Sickle,
  Melanie's Trenchcoat, Mr. Floppy (Attuned), Patsy's Sawed-Off Shotgun, Paula's Spiked Dice.
- **5 Signature Action cards:** Bribe, Combo, Kick, Prescience, Will to Live.
  Same setup rule as 1.4. Text **unknown**.
- **5 Vehicle Action cards:** Bone Chariot, Bulldozer, Chopper, Forklift, Van.
  Text **unknown**. They likely tie to the vehicle items in Feature Films (e.g.
  Bulldozer Keys, Chopper Keys, Forklift Keys, Bone Key), but don't state that as a rule.
- 3 Proxy Item cards and 15 Proxy Event cards: purpose **unknown**.
- 11 Secret Envelopes (the sealed unlockable-item envelopes, opened when you win with
  that Final Girl, as in the core rules).

### 2.1 Carolyn's Revenge (Sequel Character pack)
Official description from Series 3 Kickstarter update #6:
- A teenage Carolyn (from Creech Manor) hunts the Poltergeist, but **the pack can be
  used against ANY Killer at ANY Location.**
- It adds new Action cards for psychic attacks, teleporting, and psychically attuning
  weapons and items so they deal psychic damage to the Killer.
- **Alternate win condition:** build up enough psychic damage on the Killer, then
  succeed with the **Psychic Expulsion** Action card to banish it.
- Includes Action, Item and Final Girl cards, plus a Secret Envelope for Carolyn.
- **Mr. Floppy (Attuned)** (wiki): Carolyn only, 1 hand. You may resolve the Psychic
  Attack Action card at range 0–2. You may discard Mr. Floppy to remove the Dark
  Power from the game.
- Exact text of the Action cards and the damage threshold: **unknown**.

### 2.2 Grandma's Grudge (Character pack)
- Grandma takes the fight to the Big Bad Wolf (expansion for *Once Upon a Full Moon*).
- **Grandma's Handbag** (wiki): Grandma only. Range 1, Damage 1, 1 hand. Discard during
  the Action phase and lose 3 Time to take any Item card that was discarded or set
  aside during the game.
- Pack-specific rules: **unknown**.

### 2.3 Promo Final Girls (wiki)
- **Jamie:** 5 Health, 5 Victims to unlock. Save benefits: −1 Horror (×2), Move 1,
  +1 Time, +1 Health; extra saves: Move 1. Ultimate "Two Star Queen!": whenever a
  Horror Roll's final result is exactly 2 successes, deal 1 damage to any Enemy
  (it doesn't need to be in your space). Partials converted to successes don't count.
- **Paula** (original Series 2 promo; Holiday version in Guest Stars): 5 Health, 5 Victims.
  Save benefits: take any top Item, −1 Horror, +2 Time, +1 Health, move up to 2;
  extra saves: +2 Time. Ultimate "Things are getting dicey": whenever you'd roll
  only 1 die, roll 2 instead; if a single number is needed, you pick which die.
- **Julie** (Julie Premium Promo Pack): card text **unknown**.

## 3. Bonus Features Boxes (Series 1, 2, 3) — retailer descriptions

- Each contains a **Feature Film Rulebook** gathering the rules for all five Feature
  Films in that series, plus **Achievement lists**.
- The Series 3 box adds **5 Epic Finale cards**, one per Series 3 Killer (H.U.N.T.E.R.,
  Razorface, Slayer, Tormentor, Eyeless).
- Also used as storage for that series' Lore Book and Gruesome Death Books.
- **Build note:** if the app tracks achievements, these lists are their source.
  The Series 3 Epic Finales should appear wherever Epic Finales are offered for those Killers.

## 4. Lore & Scenario Books (Series 1, 2, 3)
- Backgrounds for each Killer and Final Girl, plus strategy tips for each Final Girl.
- **Scenarios:** set up a normal game but use the specific Killer, Location, Final Girl
  and other elements the scenario lists. Some scenarios also add special rules.
- Each Killer has a run of scenarios. Some feature a specific Final Girl, some are
  chronological, some are linked by theme. Playing them in order is recommended,
  but any can be played on its own.
- Requires the matching Feature Films. The wiki lists scenario names, e.g.
  "The Girl Must Die", "Honor Thy Father", "Terror in the Groves" (Barbara's).

## 5. Gruesome Death Books (free PDFs, one per Location)
- Official PDFs on vanrydergames.com/pages/final-girl-resources, for Series 1–4.
- Flavour text describing how characters die at that Location. No rules effect.
  Feeds the app's death-narration feature.

## 6. Other add-ons (no rules content)
- **Ultimate Boxes** (per series): storage boxes for that series' Feature Films.
- **Cast and Crew** (Series 1 and 2): storage boxes.
- **Mystery Boxes** (Series 1 and 2 Kickstarter exclusives): contents not published.
- **Miniatures / Special FX crates**: cosmetic. Vehicle miniatures stand in for vehicle tokens.

## 7. Errata worth adding
- **Legions of Hell** (Berith's Epic Finale, *A Demon in the Shadows*): the first print run
  shipped with a Correction Pack. Corrected card: Berith begins the Killer phase with
  "Target Final Girl or Victim > Move > Attack > Target Final Girl or Victim > Move > Attack",
  and when a Victim is killed, +1 Horror. The original printing wrongly split this into
  two lines, as if there were a Killer and Minions.
