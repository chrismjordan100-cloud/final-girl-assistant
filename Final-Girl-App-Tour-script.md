# Final Girl Rules Assistant — video script (v3)

## Opening
If you're anything like me, you love Final Girl. But every Killer and every Location brings its own rules, its own setup and its own exceptions, and juggling them gets in the way of actually playing.  
So what if your phone could carry all of that for you? Every rule, card and reminder you need, at the moment you need it. That's the Final Girl Rules Assistant.  

## The Game
Final Girl is a solo horror board game from Van Ryder Games. You are the last survivor in a slasher film. Rescue the victims, find a weapon, and destroy the Killer, before it destroys you.  
The Core Box holds the player board, dice and Action cards. Each Feature Film box adds one Killer and one Location, and any Killer can hunt in any Location.  
Your board tracks your health, your Horror, which sets how many dice you roll, and the Time you spend on every action.  
Each Killer brings a board, a Bloodlust track, Dark Powers and a Finale. Each Location brings its own map, Item decks, Events and Terror cards.  
And then come the special rules. The Evomorph evolves, vanishes and ambushes. The Konrad adds Keycards, maintenance shafts and a self-destruct. Across three series, that's a great deal to remember in the dark.  

## Home
The home screen is your way in. Start Setup, track a turn, or jump straight to any of the fifteen tabs.  
Every screen reshapes itself around the Killer, Location and Final Girl you pick. So we'll follow three very different nights. Laurie against Hans the Butcher at Camp Happy Trails. Ellen against the Evomorph aboard the USS Konrad. And Selena, facing the Poltergeist in the Sacred Groves: a pairing the game never intended.  

## Setup Assist
Setup Assist walks you through the whole setup, and remembers where you are. For Hans at Camp Happy Trails, there's no special setup at all.  
Switch to the Evomorph on the Konrad, and a new section appears. Start Evolution at Hatchling. Build an eight-card Terror deck, not ten. Prepare the Ambush deck. Place the Keycards, the Super Loader and the Maintenance cards.  
Choose the Poltergeist in the Groves, and it changes again. Hide Carolyn and Mister Floppy in the Item decks, because the only way to win is to rescue Carolyn. Then set up Divine Wrath. Further down, the Item deck step now carries a warning.  
Even the Setup card you record comes from your Location's own list.  
And all of this lives on your phone, propped beside the board while you lay out the components.  

## This Game
This Game gathers everything for tonight's pairing: art and flavour, your Final Girl's card, tips, icons and the rule sheets. For Hans, there are no special rules to remember.  
For the Evomorph on the Konrad, it's a very different page. It recognises the official scenario, and warns you about mistakes you've repeated before. Then it spells out the special rules in play. Evolution. Vanishing and Ambush. Scanning. Keycards, shafts and the self-destruct. Fourteen tips for this exact matchup follow, then the icons and the rule sheets.  
Mix and match, and it still keeps up. It flags that this isn't the canon pairing, then merges both rule sets. She can't be damaged, and you only win by escaping with Carolyn. While the Groves add Divine Wrath, where every increase also fires a second Bloodlust track.  

## Turn Tracker
The Turn Tracker is your live dashboard at the table. Horror, Bloodlust, Time and Victims saved sit up top, and it works out your dice. Horror three means roll two. Each of the five phases shows what to do, and the reminders people forget.  
Here, the pairing really matters. With the Konrad in play, the Action phase gains a Keycard beat.  
In the Killer phase: while the Evomorph has vanished, skip the Killer Action, and draw an Ambush card instead.  
In the Groves, once the Finale is revealed, unleash Divine Wrath, then increase it by one.  
Logging events takes a tap or two. Tap Terror card, and it offers only the cards that can be in your deck, each with a preview of its effect. Hans's butcher and his campers. The Evomorph's ambushes. The Poltergeist's hauntings, and the angry gods of the Groves.  
Pick one, and its full effect appears, so you resolve it correctly.  
Dark Powers work the same way, including the Konrad's own Slippery.  
Items come from the Location's own decks. Note a mistake as it happens, and when the night is over, one button files the lot.  
At the table, it sits in your hand. Counters pinned at the top, the phase's beats beneath, and any card's effect one tap away.  

## Codex
The Codex lets you browse every Killer, Location and Final Girl across all three series.  
Filter by name, and open At a Glance for the key rules.  
The Final Girls tab shows both sides of every card, so you can choose who suits the Killer you face.  

## Ask a Question
Ask a Question takes plain English, works offline, and tailors its answer to your game.  
Ask how Keycards work, and the Konrad's own rules, setup and tips come first, each with its source.  
Ask how to win against the Poltergeist, and it leads with the altered win condition.  
Mid-game, when you can't quite remember a rule, the answer is already in your pocket.  

## Game Log
The Game Log is your body count: every game, and whether you walked away. Open one, and you get The Story, a slasher-style recap written from the events you logged. Below it, the summary and the turn-by-turn timeline.  

## Scenarios
Scenarios is the Killer-by-Location grid. Green is beaten. Red is played, not yet beaten. Official pairings are starred.  

## MI & Outcomes
The M I tab turns your history into numbers: win rate, streaks, your best and worst matchups, and win rates by Killer, Location and Final Girl.  
After the game, it tells the story of the night, and keeps the score.  

## Achievements
Achievements tracks every Killer and Location achievement. The ones your wins prove fill in by themselves.  

## Debrief
Debrief groups your mistakes, so repeat offenders rise to the top, and pulls out the ones to watch for in this matchup.  

## Tips
Tips holds strategy advice, including the rules people most often get wrong,  
and opens on whichever Killer and Location you're playing.  

## Rules Reference
Rules Reference summarises the core rules in rulebook order, with page numbers,  
and you can search a word like Bloodlust to narrow it down.  

## Icon Reference
Icon Reference decodes every core card symbol.  
Below are the scenario icons for every Killer and Location. The Birds. The Groves' Divine Wrath. The Evomorph's Vanish and Scan. The Konrad's Keycards. Wolfe Asylum's pills. The Tormentor's death traps. The Hellscape's tiles.  

## Asset Admin
And Asset Admin keeps track of the artwork and the development backlog.  

## Close
Less time in the rulebook. More time in the dark. That's the Final Girl Rules Assistant. Now the only thing you need to worry about, is the Killer.
